Survival Guide & Controls

Objectives & Boss Fight

  • 4-Stage Labyrinth & Boss: Survive 4 escalating stages. On Stage 4, confront The Abyssal Warden who telegraphs lethal shockwave slams and Shadow Surges.
  • Character Skills [V]: Every skin grants a unique Active ([V]) or Automatic Passive power—full-map scan, 20s stun strike, sprint overdrive, auto-cloak, or toxic immunity.
  • Environmental Hazards: Higher stages trigger temporary Lab Power Outages and Toxic Gas Leaks.

Hazards & Stealth

  • Non-Fatal Hits & Smoke Bombs: Creature strikes deal damage instead of instant loss; drop a Smoke Bomb [Q] to blind hunters in its cloud.
  • Slow Crouch & Alcoves: Toggle C for slow, quiet stealth, or press E in alcoves to hide or disarm traps.
